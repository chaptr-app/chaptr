Privacy

Privacy policy.

Effective: 2026-05-15

Plain-English version: we store the minimum needed to run the app, you can export or wipe your data anytime, and Claude never sees content from your private shelves or reviews.

What we store

  • Locally on your device: reading sessions, shelves, reviews, custom challenges, AI settings, your theme. This stays in your browser even if you never sign in.
  • On our backend (only if you enable cloud sync): a copy of the above keyed under a per-device anonymous ID, and—if you sign in—your Clerk user ID, username, and display name.
  • Public data you opt into: reviews you mark as "Public", shelves you mark as "Public" or "Friends", and follow relationships.

What we never store

  • Email or password — Clerk handles auth and we never see your credentials.
  • Payment info — there's no payment.
  • Reading content from books — we store page numbers and minutes, not text.
  • Location data.
  • Device identifiers beyond a localStorage random ID.

What Claude (Anthropic) sees

  • Your aggregated reading-DNA stats (WPM, top genre, session length, mood pattern) when generating Reader Persona, "How this fits you" blurbs, stall-recovery tips, or chapter recaps.
  • Your search query when you use Ask Claude.
  • Book titles and authors for context.

Claude never receives content from your private shelves, your private reviews, or your friends' data. Every AI surface is opt-out in Profile → Claude · trust & control.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — hosts our backend (Worker + D1 database).
  • Clerk — handles authentication if you sign in.
  • Anthropic — processes your AI requests (Claude API).
  • OpenLibrary — used for book metadata and covers.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter and Fraunces typefaces.

Your controls

  • Export Reader Profile downloads a JSON of all your data (Profile page).
  • Wipe Reader Profile clears history, WPM, sessions, and streak data while keeping shelves and reviews.
  • Wipe local data in Settings clears everything on this device.
  • Sign out stops cloud sync going forward.
  • To delete cloud-stored data, sign in and contact us via the GitHub issue tracker; we'll remove your row from D1.

Children

Chaptr is not directed at children under 13. If we learn we've collected data from a child under 13, we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll update the "Effective" date above when this policy changes. Material changes will get an in-app notice.
