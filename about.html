About

What Chaptr is.

A reading habit app built around the idea that time is the richest signal you can give a reader.

The wedge

Goodreads owns metadata and social proof but doesn't help you build a reading habit. Bookly nailed the timer + streak loop but has no discovery engine. Neither uses an LLM. Chaptr connects the two: every minute you spend in a book is data Claude can use to recommend what comes next, when to read it, and how to get back into it when you stall.

What you can do

  • Track sessions — start, pause, stop a real-time reading timer. Log pages and mood at the end.
  • Keep streaks — daily goal ring, consecutive-day count, weekly streak freezes Bookly never had.
  • Build shelves — three system shelves (Reading / Want / Read) plus unlimited custom shelves with privacy controls.
  • Ask Claude — natural-language search, "How this fits you" blurbs per book, weekly Reader Persona.
  • Read together — pair-read with a friend, share a chat thread, race in group challenges, see your circle's activity.
  • Cloud sync — sign in and your data follows you across devices.

How AI is used

Every AI surface is opt-out and listed in Profile → Claude · trust & control. Claude reads your aggregated reading data (pace, genre patterns, mood) but never your private shelves or reviews. Each recommendation cites the signals it used.
